Core Features

Everything Your Organization Needs

Designed to make task delegation, daily execution, and communication crystal clear across every department.

📋

Interactive Kanban Board

Organize work visually with drag-and-drop columns (Pending, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled) and dynamic status filters.

👥

Role-Based Access Control

Precise permission boundaries for Admins, Managers, and Employees. Delegation controls ensure managers only assign to active team members.

💬

Task Discussion Drawer

Communicate directly inside each task with live discussion notes, avatar timestamps, and author role indicators.

📎

File & PDF In-App Preview

Upload images, documents, and PDFs up to 5MB. Preview images with full-screen lightbox and read PDFs directly in the browser.

🔔

In-App Notification Bell

Stay informed with instant alerts when tasks are assigned, completed, updated, or commented upon with real-time badge counts.

🌓

Dark & Light Theme

Switch between crisp airy light mode and eye-friendly deep navy dark mode with automatic local storage preference saving.
